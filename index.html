How to Play

Keyboard Controls

  • ↑ or W : Move Up
  • ↓ or S : Move Down
  • ← or A : Move Left
  • → or D : Move Right
  • Space or P : Pause / Resume
  • R : Restart Game

Retro Phone Keypad

  • 2 : Move Up
  • 8 : Move Down
  • 4 : Move Left
  • 6 : Move Right
  • 5 : Confirm / Action

Game Modes

  • Classic: Hit a wall or yourself and it's Game Over.
  • Warp: Wrap around screen edges. Avoid your tail.
  • Obstacles: Grid contains brick walls you must navigate around.
